## Row A (raqft_bb): readout definition

Δo definition (pinned 2026-10-25, identical in GROOT_2026-10-15.md, GROOT_AMRAQ_2026-10-23.md, groot_raqft.py and groot_amraq.py): Δo_{l,t} is the pre-W_O output of the FP head's cross-attention in reader block l, evaluated with the student's own step-t hidden state as query over the student's backbone tokens, minus the same over the FP backbone tokens; the difference is taken in FP32 from the BF16 readouts.

Row A's loss is the mean over the 16 reader blocks × 4 flow steps of ‖W_O Δo_{l,t}‖² (the post-W_O difference), plus 0.1 × the
velocity-direction term; A-AM (GROOT_AMRAQ_2026-10-23.md) applies a different metric to the same Δo and changes nothing else.
Row A's code path for Δo is `groot_raqft.row_a_deltas` (added 2026-10-25, the training loop itself is untouched); AM-RAQ check (d)
asserts bit-identity with A-AM's `groot_amraq.readout_deltas` on 8 fixed observations.

Non-finite updates (2026-10-25): 29 of 768 updates had an infinite gradient norm and were dropped (loss finite each time); 21
distinct calibration observations, not the same set in every epoch (7 recur), 15 of them LIBERO-Spatial frames. Root cause:
diagnostic running (GROOT_AMRAQ_2026-10-23.md, follow-up §1); the mechanism and its effect on λ_DoF / s are recorded here when
known. Rows B and C (trainable-set decision of 2026-10-20) are deferred past the GR00T deadline: untested, not abandoned.

Reading. GR00T N1.7 FP is far less robust to Plus perturbations than π0.5 (618 vs 765 on the same instances; its LIBERO medium is also lower, 949 vs 970), and 2-bit PTQ costs it 13 pp here versus 6 pp for π0.5's plain-VQ row (702 vs 765). On GR00T the action head alone accounts for most of the Plus loss (−9.9 of −13.2 pp), evenly across the two perturbation types; the backbone alone costs −4.9 pp, concentrated in the camera-viewpoint instances (−7.6) with the robot-initial-state instances within noise (−2.2 [−7.3, +3.2]) — the one place where GR00T looks like π0.5 (whose backbone loss also sits in the visual perturbations). The two parts add up roughly (−9.9 − 4.9 ≈ −13.2 both). This is the opposite of the π0.5 finding (loss follows the backbone), consistent with the medium result and with the parameter split (the DiT head is half of GR00T's quantized parameters, the π0.5 expert an eighth). Consequence for the plan: for GR00T the readout-consistency fine-tune must target the action head's codebooks (reader = the DiT's own cross-attention and self-attention readouts), not only the backbone's.
