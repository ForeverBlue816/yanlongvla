# GR00T N1.7 track (evaluation-GPU plan, 2026-10-15)

Model: official per-suite `nvidia/GR00T-N1.7-LIBERO` checkpoints (shards SHA-verified), backbone config `nvidia/Cosmos-Reason2-2B`. Architecture and quantization candidates: MODEL_MAP_GROOT.md. The backbone (vision tower + LLM layers 0–15) is byte-identical in all four suite checkpoints (frozen pretrained); the action head (vl_self_attention + 32-block DiT) differs per suite.

Reading (LIBERO medium only; the Plus subset follows): unlike π0.5, where 2-bit costs nothing on LIBERO medium and the loss shows on Plus and follows the backbone, GR00T already loses 7.7 pp on standard LIBERO at 2 bits, concentrated in LIBERO-10 (223 → 180). The two hybrids each lose 2–3 pp and are statistically indistinguishable from each other; the losses are super-additive (−3.2 and −2.2 alone, −7.7 together). The pre-registered prediction "the loss follows the backbone" is not supported on LIBERO medium: here the action head (which holds 1.13 B of the 2.34 B quantized parameters, vs 0.31 B of 2.70 B in π0.5) matters as much as the backbone.
