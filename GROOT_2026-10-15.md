## Row A (raqft_bb): readout definition

Δo definition (pinned 2026-10-25, identical in GROOT_2026-10-15.md, GROOT_AMRAQ_2026-10-23.md, groot_raqft.py and groot_amraq.py): Δo_{l,t} is the pre-W_O output of the FP head's cross-attention in reader block l, evaluated with the student's own step-t hidden state as query over the student's backbone tokens, minus the same over the FP backbone tokens; the difference is taken in FP32 from the BF16 readouts.

Row A's loss is the mean over the 16 reader blocks × 4 flow steps of ‖W_O Δo_{l,t}‖² (the post-W_O difference), plus 0.1 × the
velocity-direction term; A-AM (GROOT_AMRAQ_2026-10-23.md) applies a different metric to the same Δo and changes nothing else.
Row A's code path for Δo is `groot_raqft.row_a_deltas` (added 2026-10-25, the training loop itself is untouched); AM-RAQ check (d)
asserts bit-identity with A-AM's `groot_amraq.readout_deltas` on 8 fixed observations.

Non-finite updates (2026-10-25): 29 of 768 updates had an infinite gradient norm and were dropped (loss finite each time); 21
distinct calibration observations, not the same set in every epoch (7 recur), 15 of them LIBERO-Spatial frames. Root cause
(2026-10-25, CPU reproduction of row A's step and a unit test of the trainable layer): the infinities are produced in the backward
pass of the readout term, never by the H5b term (diagnostic: 63–92 of the 432 codebook / row-scale gradient tensors non-finite on
the affected observations, 0 for H5b; over 32 reproduced steps all 2 295 non-finite gradient tensors are row scales, 17 770 inf
and 0 NaN elements, no codebook gradient overflowed on real data). Mechanism: `TrainableVQLinear.weight()` dequantizes with an FP16 round trip of the FP32
masters (`books.to(float16).float()`, same for the row scale) so that the forward reproduces the stored FP16 codebooks exactly;
autograd differentiates the two casts, so the gradient of every codebook entry and row scale passes through FP16 on its way back
to the FP32 master. A codebook entry is shared by thousands of weight positions, so its gradient is a large sum; whenever that
sum exceeds 65 504 it becomes inf (unit test: codebook gradients saturate at 65 472 and overflow, row-scale gradients overflow
already at moderate output gradients; with a straight-through round trip — identical forward values — the same gradients are
finite, up to 6.9e7). This is an implementation limitation of the trainable layer, not a property of the data: it happens on the
observations with the largest readout loss (Spatial frames at 1e4–2.5e4 vs ~3e3 typical), and which of them overflow depends on
the current masters, hence the changing set across epochs. Two consequences for row A as trained: 29 updates (3.8%) were dropped,
and every applied gradient was rounded to FP16 precision. A straight-through estimator (forward bit-identical, FP32 gradients)
removes both; whether to adopt it for A-AM only, for both rows (re-running row A, ~1 GPU-day), or for neither is recorded as a
pending user decision in GROOT_AMRAQ_2026-10-23.md. Rows B and C (trainable-set decision of 2026-10-20) are deferred past the GR00T deadline: untested, not abandoned.
