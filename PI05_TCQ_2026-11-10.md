| Hessian | H_D = H_clean + Σ_s w_s H_s, w_s = tr(H_clean) / (3 tr(H_s)); s = photometric (512 copies), geometric (256), on-policy (256); every H = E[xxᵀ] on the FP model, equal weight per observation | recipe, perturbation copies (shift_perturb stream 0, j = 1, 2, 3), seeds | H_clean is recaptured with the same code as the sources (FP model, 256 calibration observations) instead of reusing the 2-bit row's moments |

## Implementation
- `scripts/pi05_tcq.py` (private): on-policy capture, per-source Hessians, H_D, fit (envs/groot), assembly, offline, Plus. The quantized
  model runs through the accepted 2-bit row's loader and engine (INT8 embedding, FP32 AdaRMS tables, CUDA graph); only the 414 dense
  BF16 weights (original basis) and, for the fine-tuned row, the backbone norm weights are replaced.
- `scripts/pi05_tcq_raqft.py` (private): fine-tune. The BF16 weight of each trainable layer is decoded once per update; the weight
  gradients of the 8 latents are accumulated per layer in FP32 and pushed through the decode once per update with the anchor gradient.
- `slurm/pi05_gpu.sbatch`, `slurm/pi05_pool_fit.sbatch`; chain `runs/plustrain_20261008/pi05_tcq/p_chain.sh` under the drivers job.
- Artifacts: `runs/plustrain_20261008/pi05_tcq/{onpolicy_rinit,hessians,fit,raqft,offline,plus}`, `models/pi05_tcq/<row>`.
