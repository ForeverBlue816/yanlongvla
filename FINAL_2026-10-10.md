## F2 addendum (2026-10-11)

Two novelty controls added with the same trainable set, data (a), budget and β: `raqft_kvmse` (match the prefix KV cache directly: relative squared error per layer, K and V, valid tokens) and `raqft_nowo` (readout consistency with the identity metric instead of W_O). They isolate whether the gain comes from reading the KV through the expert's queries and from the W_O weighting, rather than from KV matching per se. `raqft_beta0` is kept but is expected to be a null ablation (the β term is numerically inert, E088).

## Simplest-form row (2026-10-11, recorded before any result for it exists)

`raqft_simple` = plain-VQ (layer-Hessian) 2-bit backbone + RAQ-FT v2 with the quantized model's own expert queries (no action-Fisher calibration, no FP-expert trajectory); same data (a), budget, β and per-group learning rates. Rationale for adding it: the two component ablations (`raqft_plainvq` 748, `raqft_studentq` 749) each removed one costly ingredient without loss, so the combination is the simplest candidate method. It is chosen on this principle, not on its score: whether it replaces the frozen `raqft_a_2bit` as the paper's main backbone is decided only after the novelty controls (`raqft_kvmse`, `raqft_nowo`) report, and any replacement is confirmed exclusively on data not used for selection (the added 1000 Plus instances, LIBERO full 2000/1900, and a second quantization seed). Differences of a few successes on the 500+500 selection subset are within noise and are not a selection criterion.

## Novelty controls reported (2026-10-13)

`raqft_nowo` 744 (+4.0 [+0.78, +7.42]) and `raqft_kvmse` 725 (+2.1 [−0.88, +5.26], gate not passed; lowest KV error of all rows, E107). The gain survives removing the W_O metric and disappears when the same parameters are trained to match the KV cache instead of the readout. The paper's mechanism claim is therefore "readout consistency through the expert's queries", not "KV-cache fidelity" and not the W_O weighting.

Main backbone decision: `raqft_a_2bit` stays frozen as the main row and `a8_raqft` as the final deployable row (both already confirmed on LIBERO full and, for a8_raqft, on the expanded Plus set in progress). `raqft_simple` (748 on the selection subset) is queued for the expanded-Plus confirmation (added 1000) at the same priority as the other final-stage rows; it is presented as the simplified recipe only if its added-1000 paired difference vs original 2-bit has a CI lower bound above zero and it is not below `raqft_a_2bit` by more than the CI half-width on the same instances. No other row is promoted on selection-subset numbers.

## Full-Plus confirmation of the backbone decision (user, 2026-10-13)

The user's instruction: the only thing the main backbone still needs is Fisher-weighted calibration versus none on the complete LIBERO-Plus; using the quantized model's own expert queries is accepted. Rows: `raqft_a_2bit` (action-Fisher calibration, FP-expert queries; frozen main row) and `raqft_simple` (plain layer-Hessian VQ, quantized-model queries) on all 10,030 instances (seven perturbation categories), paired directly; FP on the full set as the reference (reference priority; the 2-bit base is not rolled out on the full set). Decision rule, fixed before the rows exist: if `raqft_simple` is not below `raqft_a_2bit` on the full set by more than the paired CI half-width, the paper's main recipe becomes the simplest form (no Fisher calibration, no FP-expert trajectory) and `raqft_a_2bit` is reported as the Fisher variant; otherwise the frozen row stays and the simplest form is an ablation. Either way the final deployable row (W2A8) is re-composed only on the chosen backbone; A8 has been shown free on two backbones. Expanded-Plus result for the frozen row (E108): added 1000, +3.2 pp vs 2-bit [−0.03, +6.62].
