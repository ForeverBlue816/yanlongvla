## Specification (user, verbatim)

> TCQ base, built in our own pipeline, + RAQ-FT on GR00T (2026-11-01). We do not use Chamadol's QTIP checkpoints. We implement trellis-coded quantization (TCQ) as a new base format inside our quantizer, fit it with our calibration data, Hessians and rotation, evaluate it as PTQ, then apply our readout-consistency fine-tune. Pre-register in TCQ_RAQFT_2026-11-01.md before the first job.
>
> ## 0. Format (fixed)
> - Rate: 2 bits per weight. Trellis: L = 16 state bits, V = 2 (two weights per trellis symbol), K = 2 bits per weight, "hybrid" code: a per-layer lookup table of 2^9 entries (tlut, FP16) indexed by a bit-shift hash of the trellis state, exactly the structure of the QTIP HYB code (Tseng et al. 2024). You may use the QTIP open-source quantizer (Viterbi + bitshift trellis + LUT) as the encoding engine; everything around it is ours.
> - Rotation: our block-Hadamard incoherence transform with the same blocks as the current base (1536 = 3×512, 6144 = 3×2048, 4304 = 4096+128+64+16, etc.), same seeds.
> - Scales: per-output-row FP16 scale fitted by our WLS, as in the current base.
> - Fit objective: layer Hessian H_in from our 256-observation calibration bank a, LDLQ-style column feedback with Viterbi inside each 16×16 tile (QTIP's td_x = td_y = 16), 1% mean-diagonal damping, retry at 10× / 100× on Cholesky failure and record it per layer (the LLM layer-2 down_proj needs it).
> - Storage counted: 2-bit trellis codes + tlut (1 KB/layer) + row scales + rotation seeds. Target linear bpw ≈ 2.00–2.03, whole-model with the same accounting as ptq_w2 (report both). Nothing else in the model changes: same quantized layer set (216 + 216), BF16 for proj_out / encoders / embeddings, AdaLN tables precomputed per flow step.
> - Name: groot_tcq_w2.
>
> ## 1. Correctness gates before any rollout
> (a) Encoder check: on 3 layers (LLM attention, LLM MLP, DiT cross-attn), the Hessian-weighted error of our TCQ fit must be below our 2×8 RVQ fit for the same layer; report the ratio (expected 0.7–0.8). If it is not lower, stop and report.
> (b) Round trip: encode → export in our payload format → decode → forward on 2 observations matches the fake-quant forward bit-for-bit.
> (c) Rotation / tiling: every layer's dims are handled (padding, if any, is counted in payload_bytes and reported).
> (d) Speed: encoding time per layer; the full 432-layer fit must fit in ≤ 6 GPU-h, otherwise reduce the Viterbi beam only in the DiT and record it.
>
> ## 2. PTQ evaluation
> Held-out action MSE (total, per DoF) and readout error vs ptq_w2 (base) and FP. Then Plus selection 1000, paired vs our base 486, FP 618, and Chamadol's QTIP 573 (his per-unit file, same units and seeds; record GPU). Camera / robot-init split. This row answers "is our TCQ fit as good as QTIP's"; if it is more than 3 pp below 573, audit the fit before fine-tuning (compare per-layer errors with his table).
>
> ## 3. RAQ-FT on the TCQ base (B2 recipe, adapted)
> Trainable per layer: tlut (FP32 master, stored-dtype round trip), row scales, RMSNorm / LayerNorm weights. Frozen: trellis codes, rotation signs, everything that affects bpw.
> Loss and setup exactly as B2: readouts inside the student's own 2-bit head (DiT cross-attention outputs before W_O, student queries) vs the FP head on the FP trajectory, W_O metric; plus the velocity term with β set at step 0 by gradient-norm matching (DiT-side vs backbone-side, within 10×); prefix + head trainable; one shared backbone trained jointly against the four suite heads; bank a, 8 latents, 768 updates, Adam with B2's per-group learning rates (tlut uses the codebook group), clip 1, final iterate. Gates before step 1: tlut and scale gradients non-zero in every layer, DiT-side gradient norm > 1e-4.
> Name: groot_tcq_raqft.
>
> ## 4. Evaluation of the fine-tuned row
> Held-out as in §2. Plus selection 1000 paired vs groot_tcq_w2, QTIP 573, B2 556, base 486, FP 618. Then LIBERO medium 1000 paired vs FP 949 and base 872.
> Reading (fixed now): groot_tcq_raqft is the GR00T main row if its paired gain over its own PTQ row has CI lower bound > 0 and it is at least level with QTIP 573. If RAQ-FT adds nothing on the TCQ base, say so; B2 stays the row for our RVQ format and the paper reports both formats.
>
> ## 5. Optional, in order, only if §4 passes
> (a) W2A8 on groot_tcq_raqft (per-token INT8 activations after the folded rotation), held-out, Plus selection 1000.
> (b) The same TCQ base + raqft_studentq recipe on π₀.₅ (pi05_tcq_w2 → pi05_tcq_raqft), paired vs 704 / 749 / 765 and Chamadol's QTIP π₀.₅ row when it exists.

A method paragraph for the paper: TCQ as the base (why 2-bit TCQ sits within ~0.2 dB of the Gaussian rate–distortion bound while 8-dim codebooks cannot exceed 0.65 dB of the 1.53 dB granular gain), what is frozen, what RAQ-FT trains, and the block-Hadamard handling.

## Implementation (fixed before the first job)

**Format and fit (`groot_tcq.py --stage fit`).** Per Linear W (m × n): (1) our RHT (`rht.layer_signs(name)` + block Walsh of
`backbone_rht.block_widths(n)`: 2048, 1536 = 1024 + 512, 6144 = 4096 + 2048, 4096, 1024, 8192, 3072 = 2048 + 1024; the spec's
"1536 = 3×512, 6144 = 3×2048" are not the base's blocks — the base's maximal power-of-two blocks are kept, same seeds, so the
rotation is bit-identical to ptq_w2's; GR00T has no 4304-wide layer) on W and H (`transform`, `transform_covariance`); (2) per-row
scale s_r = RMS(W'_r) / 0.9 in FP16 (QTIP's `scale_override` 0.9 as the starting point); (3) H' normalised by its mean diagonal,
+ 0.01 I (1 % mean-diagonal damping), `block_LDL(·, 16)` → on Cholesky failure retry at 0.1 I then 1.0 I, the damping used is
recorded per layer; (4) `LDLQ` with `buf_cols = 16` (so every width that is a multiple of 16 is tiled exactly: all 432 GR00T widths
are — no padding; `for_kernel = False`, no kernel permutation), the bitshift codebook L 16 / K 2 / V 2 / `quantlut_sym` / 2^9-entry
tlut; the initial tlut is the QTIP Gaussian k-means table (1 M N(0,1)² samples, seed 0, unit variance × 0.968), built once in
`OUT/groot/tcq/tlut_init.pt` instead of QTIP's `/tmp` cache; (5) **our WLS on the row scales** with the fixed codes: s_r ←
argmin_s (W'_r − s Q_r) H' (W'_r − s Q_r)ᵀ = (W'_r H' Q_rᵀ) / (Q_r H' Q_rᵀ), rounded to FP16; (6) **our WLS on the per-layer tlut**
with the fixed codes and scales: the 1024 table values enter the reconstruction linearly (each weight = ± one tlut entry), the
normal equations use the full H' (accumulated per row), accepted only if the calibration H-error decreases (as the base's codebook
WLS); (7) one re-encode (LDLQ) with the refit tlut and scales, then steps 5–6 again; the deployed round is the one with the
smallest calibration H-weighted error (as `vq_fit.fit_uniform` selects among its rounds). Rounds are reduced to encode + (5) + (6)
if gate (d) requires it (recorded). `torch.compile` of the Viterbi state update is tried on the GPU and disabled
(`TORCH_COMPILE_DISABLE=1`) if it fails, recorded.

**Payload per layer** (`payload_bytes`): packed trellis m·n/4 B (exactly 2 bits/weight: `pack_trellis` keeps T·K·V bits per tile
sequence, no padding at these widths) + tlut 2^9 × 2 × FP16 = 2 048 B (the spec's "1 KB/layer" counts 512 entries; the V = 2 table
is 2 KB) + row scales 2m B + rotation signs n B (int8, as the base counts them; the seed would be 8 B). Expected linear bpw:
2.0 + (2048 + 2m + n)·8/(m·n) ≈ 2.001–2.01 per layer; model-level 2.003 (base 2.023); whole-model with `stage_assemble`'s
accounting (retained BF16 tensors 1 607 188 736 B unchanged).

**Record format** (so `groot_ptq.stage_assemble`, `groot_offline` and the Plus stage work unchanged): `OUT/groot/ptq/fit/<key>/<layer>.pt`
= `{w_hat (BF16, original basis), tensors: {trellis (uint16 packed), tlut (FP16 512×2), row_scale (FP16 m), signs (int8 n)},
tcq: {L, K, V, tlut_bits, td}}`, `.json` with `payload_bytes`, `relative_weight_mse`, `hessian_relative_error` (same metric as the
base: tr(ΔW H ΔWᵀ)/tr(W H Wᵀ) in the original basis), `damping`, `cholesky_retries`, `rounds`, `seconds`. Keys `backbone_tcq`,
`head_tcq_<suite>`; row `tcq_w2 = ('backbone_tcq', 'head_tcq')`.

**Decode (`groot_tcq.decode`)**: unpack trellis → `recons` through the 2^9 tlut (sign flip on the first coordinate per QTIP's
`quantlut_sym`) → tiles → × FP16 row scale → inverse RHT → BF16. Gate (b) compares the decoded weights with the fit records'
`w_hat` bit for bit, and the actions of the assembled checkpoint (`models/groot_ptq/tcq_w2/libero_spatial`) on 2 held-out
observations with the actions of the same model with the decoded weights loaded, bit for bit (same seed, same device).

**Gate (a)**: layers `backbone.model.model.language_model.layers.8.self_attn.q_proj` (LLM attention),
`backbone.model.model.language_model.layers.8.mlp.down_proj` (LLM MLP), `action_head.model.transformer_blocks.8.attn2.to_q`
(DiT cross-attention), libero_spatial head; ratio = TCQ `hessian_relative_error` / base `hessian_relative_error` from the base
fit JSONs; pass iff every ratio < 1. Also reported: the LLM layer-2 `mlp.down_proj` (damping retries).

**Fine-tune (`groot_tcq.py --stage raqft`, row `tcq_raqft`)**: `groot_rowb2.run` with the TCQ base: `TrainableTCQLinear`
(buffers: signed int16 tlut index per weight (sign in the sign bit), RHT signs; parameters `books` = tlut FP32 master (512 × 2) with
the FP16 round trip in the forward, `scale` = row scales FP32 master with B2's straight-through FP16 round trip) installed from the
`tcq` fit keys, B2's optimizer groups (tlut in the codebook group, lr 3e-4; row scales 2e-5), plus every RMSNorm / LayerNorm weight
of the backbone and the four heads as a third group at the row-scale learning rate 2e-5 (the spec fixes the set, not the rate;
norm weights are multiplicative gains like the scales — recorded here as the agent's choice). Gates before step 1: non-zero tlut
and scale gradient in every trainable layer (first batch), DiT-side gradient norm > 1e-4 (B2's gate). Export keys
`backbone_tcq_raqft`, `head_tcq_raqft_<suite>`; row `tcq_raqft`.

**Readout error (§2, §4)**: B2's L_readout (student-head readouts vs the FP head on the FP trajectory, W_O metric) averaged over the
256 held-out observations with noise seed 0, computed by `groot_tcq.py --stage readout` for `ptq_w2`, `tcq_w2`, `tcq_raqft` (and FP =
0 by definition).

**Chamadol's QTIP per-unit file (573)**: not in the repository (`baselines/groot/results/` holds only our FP / PTQ / B2 files). The
paired comparison vs 573 is computed as soon as his `plus_1000.jsonl` is available; until then the Δ vs 573 is the difference of
totals. Requested from the user.

**Reading rules** are the spec's (§2: > 3 pp below 573 → audit the fit before fine-tuning; §4: main row iff paired gain over
`groot_tcq_w2` has CI lower bound > 0 and ≥ 573).

**Deadlines** (Beijing → UTC): §1 by 2026-11-02 10:00 UTC; §2 Plus by 2026-11-03; §3–4 by 2026-11-05.
