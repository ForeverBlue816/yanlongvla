Paired bootstrap over (task, layout) clusters, stratified by task (`simpler/b5_stats.py`). FP run twice with the same seed gives the same result on 809 of 840 episodes (H3), so the intervals are not too narrow. QuIP# 0 sweeps is above FP (+7.4) and above QTIP (+4.0 [+1.3, +6.8]); VPTQ is above FP (+7.3) and not separated from QuIP# (-0.1 [-3.3, +3.0]) or QTIP (+3.9 [-0.1, +8.0]); LIBERO's QTIP above VPTQ is not reproduced on Bridge. QTIP is not separated from FP. The gains come from carrot_on_plate and stack_cube, where FP is exactly reproducible and all 2-bit rows succeed far more often, and they are partly paid back on put_eggplant_in_basket. This is the opposite of LIBERO (every 2-bit row below FP); H8 below explains it. AQLM is not separated from FP (+2.9); its raw intervals against QuIP# (-4.5 [-8.6, -0.7]) and VPTQ (-4.4 [-8.6, -0.2]) exclude 0, but with Holm over the 10 Bridge pairs (audit A4, `explore/a4a8/out.txt`, 8 Nov) only three pairs stay separated: QuIP# and VPTQ above FP, and QuIP# above QTIP; so AQLM is not ordered against any row, and LIBERO's AQLM below QTIP is not reproduced. On the 5 tasks other than carrot_on_plate and stack_cube, FP is above QTIP (+4.3 [+2.0, +6.8], Holm-separated) and not separated from QuIP#, VPTQ or AQLM. All four 2-bit rows gain on carrot_on_plate (+12 to +32) and stack_cube (+26 to +45). A matched random-noise control (H4: Gaussian noise of 6% and 2% of each weight's rms on the same 432 linear weights, FP otherwise) is mixed, so the cause is not settled: one random draw moves carrot_on_plate or stack_cube by 10 to 16 episodes in either direction (6%, seed 0: carrot -11, stack +12; seed 1: carrot +16, stack -4; 2%: -3, +9), and no draw raised both tasks. With 6 draws at 6% (H5), stack_cube moves from -23 to +23 and carrot from -29 to +16 around FP; every 2-bit row's stack_cube gain (+26 to +45) is larger than each draw (a weak test: about 1 in 7 by chance per row; the draws are also about 1.35x smaller than the 2-bit error in the Hessian-weighted measure), and no draw raises both tasks by 10 or more. Without carrot_on_plate and stack_cube, no 2-bit row is above FP on the other five tasks (QTIP 366 against 392 of 600), so the Bridge gain is a two-task effect. Checks (GOAL v7, 8 Nov): NVIDIA's own Bridge command (100 unseeded episodes, 5 envs, flash-attn) on our stack reproduces NVIDIA's FP on every task (61.5% against 62.3%; stack_cube 45 against 48; H6), but (H9, 9 Nov) NVIDIA's command walks one fixed layout chain (seed 2022), and with a fresh chain per run it gives stack_cube 33.8% (6 x 100), the same as the mentor's seeded protocol; so the low stack_cube is the policy, not the protocol; under that protocol FP stays at 30 to 37% when only numerics change (flash-attn, eval seed 8; H7). Under NVIDIA's command QuIP# 0 sweeps keeps its gains: carrot_on_plate 86.0% against 58.7% (+27.3), stack_cube 67.3% against 45.0% (+22.3), put_eggplant_in_basket 32.7% against 49.0% (-16.3), mean 66.3% against 61.5% (+4.8, SE 1.1; unpaired, 3 x 100 episodes per task). So the Bridge gains on carrot_on_plate and stack_cube are not an artifact of the protocol or of a low FP draw; why the 2-bit rows do better on these two tasks is open (exploratory checks in EXPLORE_2026-11-08.md: the gains also hold when success is scored at the last of 300 steps). **Explanation (H8, pre-registered, 8 Nov):** the 2-bit error of every method we checked shrinks each of the 432 quantized weights a little along itself (projection -0.04 to -0.07 in all 432 tensors for QuIP#, and for QTIP on LIBERO-10; 2 to 3% of the error energy), and this shrink alone gives the gain. Multiplying the 432 FP weights by 0.95, with no quantizer (sh95), gives carrot_on_plate 102 and stack_cube 69 of 120 (FP 75, 36; QuIP# 107, 69): +25.0 [+15.4, +35.4] on the two tasks, on mostly the same episodes. Multiplying them by 1.05 (gr105) lowers both (53, 18): -16.7 [-25.4, -8.3], so the gain needs a shrink, not any change of scale. On all 7 tasks sh95 is 69.9% against FP 59.9% (+10.0 [+6.5, +13.8]) and is the best Bridge row: not separated from QuIP# (+2.6 [-0.7, +6.2]) or VPTQ (+2.7 [-1.0, +6.7]), above QTIP (+6.7 [+2.9, +10.6]) and AQLM (+7.1 [+2.9, +11.3]) (pointwise intervals); unlike the 2-bit rows it does not lose on put_eggplant_in_basket (78 against FP 56; this task flips 24 of 120 episodes between identical FP runs, A12). So a plain weight shrink, with no quantizer, gives the Bridge gain; the "2-bit above FP" is not evidence that quantization as such helps. But the 2-bit error is not simply a shrink of the layer outputs (X25, 9 Nov): on the calibration and held-out activations, the QuIP# error shrinks the layer outputs by only 0.2 to 0.9% (the weight-space shrink lies mostly in directions that the inputs rarely excite), and the QuIP# error with its weight-space shrink removed (`orth`, outputs about 5% larger) still gives carrot_on_plate 109 (EXPLORE X15), and with its output gain change also removed (`mxorq`, X26) carrot_on_plate 106 and stack_cube 78 of 120. So the shrink and the 2-bit error reach the same gain, and the route of the 2-bit error is not identified; FP on these two tasks is easy to improve (H4: one random draw moves a task by up to 16 episodes). Why the shrink helps (EXPLORE W5, X13, X14, X16): the gain has a broad optimum at 0.90 to 0.95 W; it comes from the weights that write into the residual stream (attention v/o: stack_cube; MLPs and the action head: carrot), not from q/k; a shrink of the writers changes the mix of embeddings and layer outputs in the residual stream, which the norms cannot undo (the backbone output turns by 27%); smaller output actions, a softer attention and other denoising step counts do not give it; a larger start noise gives a smaller part (+9 points on the two tasks). The gain also holds under NVIDIA's command with fair layouts (carrot +15.7, stack_cube +21.9 points, W6).

Configuration and FP draw (audits A15, A14, 9 Nov): the Bridge QuIP# row is QuIP# without tuning sweeps (0 sweeps), the appendix configuration of the LIBERO tables; the official 10-sweeps configuration is running on Bridge now (on GR00T LIBERO it is 2.3 points below 0 sweeps, not separated), so the Bridge QuIP# result must not be read as the official default. The seed-7 FP is 5.7 stack_cube episodes below the mean of 13 FP draws, so the Bridge 2-bit gains over FP are about 0.7 points smaller on the 7-task mean (QuIP# about +6.7).
