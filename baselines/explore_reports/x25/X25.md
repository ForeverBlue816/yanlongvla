# X25: activation-space shrink of the QuIP# 2-bit error (CPU only)

Script: x25.py. Data: x25.json (per tensor, per group). Log: log.txt.
All 432 tensors have a Hessian, both calibration (256 obs) and held-out. E = Wq - W (QuIP# w2). W is [out,in], H = E[x x^T] of the input.
c_w = <E,W>/<W,W>. c_a = tr(E H W^T)/tr(W H W^T). Orth error E_perp = E - c_w W. rel_out = tr(E H E^T)/tr(W H W^T).
Check: c_a(E_perp) = c_a - c_w holds (max deviation 1.2e-8). Orth checkpoint matches W + E - c_w W (max abs diff <= 1e-3, bf16 rounding).

## Findings
1. The output shrink c_a is NOT larger than c_w. It is 5 to 25 times smaller. |c_a| < |c_w| on all 432 tensors.
2. The weight-space shrink (c_w about -0.05) is almost absent in the output on real activations (c_a about -0.003 to -0.009). The activations avoid the directions where QuIP# shrinks W.
3. So the hypothesis is false in its direction. Removing c_w W from E does not leave a shrink. It ADDS about +4 to +5 percent output gain: c_a(orth) = +0.04 to +0.05 in every group.
4. The orth row therefore is an output EXPANSION of about 5 percent (on calibration and held-out activations alike). Output error along W x is tiny (c_a^2 about 1e-3 to 3e-3 of the output norm), the rest is orthogonal error.
5. The calibration set does not drive this. Held-out c_a values match calibration values (group means differ by < 0.004).

## Implication for orth carrot 109
- Carrot gain appears with 0.95 W (output shrink -5%), QuIP# (output shrink about -0.5%) and orth (output expansion +5%). Sign of the output scale change does not decide it.
- A pure output-scale story fails. The shared part is the orthogonal (noise-like) part of E, which has relative output error 0.1 to 0.8 percent in QuIP# and 0.3 to 1 percent in orth. Test further with a random-direction error of equal rel_out, and with an orth row that sets c_a to 0.
- Caution: 0.95 W has c_a = -0.05 exactly, so 0.95 W and orth are two opposite-sign gain changes of equal size, and both give carrot above FP (102, 109). A change of any size or sign, not shrink itself, may lift carrot.
