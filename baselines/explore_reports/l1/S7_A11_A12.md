## A12 Bridge basket determinism
- fprr is a repeat of FP: same code, same seeds (checked: layout and seed equal in all 840 episodes). All 7 tasks x 120 records complete, and .done files for fprr exist. fpfa, fpsl, fpnt, fps8 exist only for carrot_on_plate and stack_cube (no basket data).
- Flips fp vs fprr (and env_steps that differ):
  spoon_on_towel 0 (0), carrot_on_plate 0 (0), stack_cube 0 (0), put_eggplant_in_sink 0 (0; FP 0 successes, uninformative),
  put_eggplant_in_basket 24 (14 up, 10 down; 30 episodes differ in steps), open_drawer 1 (52 differ in steps), close_drawer 6 (4 up, 2 down; 51 differ in steps).
- Same-seed flips on carrot/stack for fpsl = 0 both; fpfa 29/23 and fpnt 8/1 are numerics variants (flash-attn, other change), not repeats.
- So: spoon, carrot, stack, (sink) are bitwise reproducible, including episode lengths. Basket, open_drawer, close_drawer are not. For the drawers, success is almost always 100%, flips are rare; only the step counts move.
- Basket flips spread over all 8 layouts (6,4,4,3,2,2,2,1 flips on layouts 1,7,3,4,0,2,6,5). 8 layouts, 15 episodes each. Not tied to one layout.
- Code: no unseeded RNG found in eval_simpler_bridge.py (np.random, random, ManiSkill episode seed, torch noise via simpler_server reset all seeded per episode). PutEggplantInBasketScene (ManiSkill2_real2sim put_on_in_scene.py:375) has no random call of its own: positions come from episode_id (layout) and fixed grid. It differs from carrot/stack/spoon by a locked "sink" mesh actor and an eggplant that is dropped into it (soft contact, rolling). The drawers are articulated.
- Conclusion: cause not proven. Best candidate: PhysX contact / articulation nondeterminism (thread order or float accumulation) in contact-rich scenes where outcome is near a threshold (z_flag_required_offset 0.06, no full-on-target requirement), or policy numerics that only matter when sim state is chaotic. The policy itself is deterministic in carrot/stack/spoon runs.
